Workflow

How Smart Lender Works

A transparent 4-stage predictive workflow designed to automate loan underwriting.

1. Applicant Profile

Collects demographic details, income figures, employment status, credit history, and loan requirements.

2. Data Preprocessing

Applies mode/median imputations, one-hot categorical encoding, and numerical standard scaling.

3. ML Inference

Executes trained ensemble trees (Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) to compute approval probability.

4. Decision & Insights

Delivers immediate decision, confidence score, EMI ratio, risk categorization, and persistent storage.
